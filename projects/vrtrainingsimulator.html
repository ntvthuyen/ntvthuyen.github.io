VR Competency-Based Pilot Training for Threat and Error Management

VR AVIATION TRAINING RESEARCH

Full flight simulators are expensive and limited in availability, which caps how much training capacity airlines can offer. This project — Workpackage 2 of a five-member research team — built a VR-based alternative in collaboration with Singapore Airlines, recreating a realistic approach scenario with deliberately introduced threats and errors so pilots can train in a scalable, self-directed way rather than only in scheduled simulator sessions.

I joined the team later on to lead the evaluation phase, contribute to refining and expanding the system's design, and drive the writing of the resulting papers.
