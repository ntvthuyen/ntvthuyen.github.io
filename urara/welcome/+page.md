# About me!

My name is Vinh-Thuyen. You can pronounce my name by saying “thu” and “yen” as fast as possible. My mom wanted to name me after a person named Han Thuyen (韓詮), so 詮 is the primary meaning :)


I'm a PhD student at Department of Design, College of Design and Engineering, National University of Singapore. I'm a part of <img src="/assets/i3d-palette-logo.png" alt="icon" style="width: 95px; display:  inline-block !important"> [i3d](https://www.i3d.design/) and [palette lab](https://palettelab-nus.github.io/PaletteLab/). 

I hold a BSc (APCS program) and a Msc degree in Computer Science. 

Previously, I was a Research Engineer at the SIA–NUS Corporate Laboratory at IORA, a Research Assistant at MEILab, City University of Hong Kong (CityU).

My research interests are Human–Computer Interaction, Information & Knowledge Management, Spatial Computing, Creativity Support Tools, and Community Support. I have broad experience and knowledge in artificial intelligence, software engineering, and other computer-related fields.

I love computers, Linux, coding, learning, games, Dota, Dota 2, Counter-Strike, manga, playing the guitar, math rock, running, swimming, and penguins.
